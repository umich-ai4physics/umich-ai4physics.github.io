About

AI is reshaping how physics is done: from model building, data analysis, physical inference, and even the processes by which knowledge is disseminated and passed-down. This seminar seeks to bring together researchers across physics (broadly defined) to discuss AI methodologies that are moving science forward, and the opportunities and challenges they bring.

Talks are aimed at a broad physics audience, with a solid Q&A afterward moderated by the organizers.
